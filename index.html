Questions before a project starts

Write even if the problem is not settled. That is usually the right time.

Can we talk before requirements exist?

Yes. We prefer it. We start from where you are, what the business wants, and what users struggle with.

When can you join?

Raw idea, new venture, existing product, UI rebuild, engineering, or operating model. Scope follows the actual stage.

Design only, or engineering only?

Yes. We still confirm goal, users, and the steps either side of our part so the work holds up.

AI adoption is still just an idea.

Fine. We look at whose problem it solves, where a person decides, and how it sits in the workflow.

Can you sit with our in-house team?

Yes. Roles with leadership, PM, design, and engineering. Leaving criteria inside the company is part of the job.

Time and price?

A framing or audit can be weeks; design through engineering, months. Quotes follow scope, duration, and the expertise required. If you have a budget, we prioritise inside it.

What should we bring to a first talk?

Nothing formal. What is hard, what you want, who the users are, timing and budget — as much as you know.
