# HAVU design notes

Observed from https://www.havu.cc/en (not a dump of their CSS).

- Paper `#FAF9F7` / `#F2F0ED`, ink `#121212`, accent `#155DFC`
- Type: Noto Sans JP + Roboto
- Pattern: long-scroll studio narrative — hero, problems, loop, delivery, method, team, FAQ, contact
- Motion: light only; respect `prefers-reduced-motion`
- Copy on this repo is original paraphrase, not a verbatim lift of the live site
