# HAVU design notes

Observed from https://www.havu.cc/en (not a dump of their CSS).

- Paper white `255 255 255`, wash `#FAF9F7` / `#F2F0ED` / `#F8F6F3`
- Ink `rgb(10 10 10)`, links `rgb(42 111 171)`, marker `#FFF7CA`
- Motion: `cubic-bezier(.16, 1, .3, 1)`, ~0.9s transform/opacity reveals, line-mask on the hero, header wash on scroll
